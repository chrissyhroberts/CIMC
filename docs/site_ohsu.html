5  OHSU monitoring

Recruitment, follow-up, action queues, and data quality

Data are current through 24 August 2026. This page is restricted to OHSU and uses the same study-wide configuration, denominators, and alert rules as the central dashboard.

What it does. It brings recruitment, daily and monthly completion, individual reporting patterns, follow-up queues, downloads, and data-quality checks into one operational site view.

Why it helps. Site teams can identify and manage their own follow-up problems without filtering the central dashboard or receiving records belonging to another site.
