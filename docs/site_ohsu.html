5.1 Site action lists

These are operational participant-level worklists for this site. The expandable tables are intended to make the reason for each flag and the expected follow-up clear without requiring a CSV download. The CSV files continue to be generated and routed separately for operational use and audit. In a downloaded Kobo dashboard archive, the site CSVs are stored in the actions/ folder; they are not part of the rendered website itself. When action-list publication is enabled, the same records can also be inspected in the expandable tables below.

Consecutive missed daily reports 10 records

What this list shows. Active enrolled participants at this site who currently have at least 3 consecutive due daily reports with no submission. The streak is counted backwards from the latest due day and resets when reporting resumes.

Operational use. Use this as the current reminder/outreach queue: check whether the participant needs a reminder, technical support, or another documented follow-up action.

Interpretation. This is a current-action list, not a history of every previous reporting gap. Historical gaps remain visible elsewhere in the dashboard.

What this list shows. Automated cross-form flags that may represent protocol deviations, protocol violations, or data/linkage problems. The rules compare recruitment, enrollment and CIMC records and apply the classifications configured for the study.
