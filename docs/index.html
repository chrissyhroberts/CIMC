0.1.1 Cumulative enrollment

What it shows. Actual cumulative enrollment against the straight-line plan implied by the configured recruitment start date, target date, and site targets.

How it is calculated. Enrollments are counted once on their enrollment date. The planned line rises evenly from zero to the total configured target; it is a monitoring reference rather than a statistical model.

0.1.2 Recruitment funnel

What it shows. Unique people at successive stages from screening through daily reporting and completion of the observation window.

How it is calculated. A person appears in every stage they have reached, so this is a cumulative funnel rather than a set of mutually exclusive groups.

Why it helps. The largest drop between adjacent stages identifies where recruitment or onboarding may need attention.

0.1.3 Enrollment against site targets

What it shows. Enrollment achieved at each site relative to its configured target.

How it is calculated. Enrolled participants are counted once at their enrollment site and divided by that site’s target in dashboard.yml.

Why it helps. It distinguishes overall recruitment success from site-specific delivery. It does not measure reporting quality after enrollment.

0.2.1 Participant progress

What it shows. The current follow-up status of enrolled participants within each site.

How it is calculated. Status uses the configured observation window and method-discontinuation responses. “Reached day 90” means the time window elapsed; it does not require perfect questionnaire completion.

Why it helps. It separates participants who remain under active follow-up from those who have completed, stopped, or never begun daily reporting.

0.2.2 Daily completion trend

What it shows. The proportion of expected daily reports actually received in each site-week.

How it is calculated. Each point divides received participant-days by expected participant-days among people under observation that week.

Why it helps. It separates growth in the enrolled cohort from genuine reporting adherence and makes site-specific implementation changes visible over time.

Daily participant tracking — most recent 14 days

What it shows. A compact recent completion calendar for the active participants most in need of attention. Green cells are received daily forms, coral cells are missed due forms, and grey cells were not due.

How it is calculated. Rows prioritise participants meeting the consecutive-miss rule and then those with the lowest overall daily completion. The number of displayed rows and days is configurable. Participant identifiers are shown only when publication of action lists is enabled.

Monthly participant tracking — all study months

How it is calculated. Monthly due dates use the configured day of month and each participant’s observed follow-up interval. Rows with the most missing monthly sections appear first within each site.

0.3 Potential protocol deviations and violations

What it shows. This section screens linked study forms for records that may represent a protocol deviation or violation. The supplied rules include CIMC reports without enrollment, missing programme start dates, reports dated before enrollment, duplicate participant-days, duplicate enrollments, and enrollments without recruitment records.

How it is calculated. Exact participant IDs and recorded dates are compared across recruitment, enrollment, and CIMC submissions. Each rule and its provisional classification are controlled in dashboard.yml. CIMC submissions without a valid linked programme start remain in this monitoring register but are excluded from symptom and cycle analyses.

Why it helps. A linkage problem should create an actionable signal rather than prevent the dashboard from rendering. These are potential events: the dashboard does not determine seriousness, causality, or reportability. The study team must verify source records and adjudicate each flag under the approved protocol and deviation-management procedures.

0.5 Completion-pattern UpSet chart

What it shows. Exact combinations of reporting and follow-up milestones reached by participants.

How it is calculated. The upper bars count participants in each combination; filled dots identify the milestones included in that combination.

Why it helps. It shows whether apparent progress is driven by complete follow-up, strong daily adherence, monthly completion, or only one of those elements.
